# About Make Me Cartoon

Make Me Cartoon turns your favourite photos into cartoons, illustrated portraits, collectible toys, new scenes, restored memories and family collages, right on your iPhone.

## About Karma Academy
Make Me Cartoon is made by Karma Academy, an Australian-registered business based in Brisbane. Karma Academy develops apps for Apple platforms.
